# Sellier & Bellot Czech component table

- Status: answered
- Date: 2026-10-27
- Topic: Licensing

## Question

Sellier & Bellot publishes component bullets. May the rifle rows be committed?

## Context

Checked 2026-10-27. The US legal policy at [sellierbellot.us/contact/legal-policy](https://www.sellierbellot.us/contact/legal-policy/) says the user agrees not to copy, reproduce, publish, or store any content on www.sellierbellot.us without express prior written consent. That site was not used.

The rifle component table is on [sellier-bellot.cz/en/products/components/bullets](https://www.sellier-bellot.cz/en/products/components/bullets/). The Czech site footer and company page have no terms link and no reproduction ban. The footer says Sellier & Bellot a.s. is part of Colt CZ Group SE. The rifle table prints product number, bullet number, type, grains, grams, caliber, a millimeter diameter, an inch diameter, and G1. The pistol and revolver table on the same page is omitted. A caliber printed as `7,62*` has no footnote on the page.

## Working assumption until answered

Commit the rifle rows from the Czech table. Leave the US site unread. Schema stays 1.2.0.

## Answer

The Czech component page does not forbid copying. The rifle rows are committed. The US site is not used. Schema stays 1.2.0.
