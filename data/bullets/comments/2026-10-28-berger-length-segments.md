# Berger lengths that do not add up

- Status: answered
- Date: 2026-10-28
- Topic: Nose, bearing surface, and boattail versus overall length

## Question

BOTS.md (2026-10-28, late) asked for a re-check of four Berger records whose nose length, bearing surface, and boattail length differ from overall length by more than 0.05 in: 30 cal 208 gr LR Hybrid Target, the two 375 ELR Match Solids, and 6.5 mm 130 gr VLD Target.

## Context

Each product page was read again on 2026-10-28. The stored numbers are the numbers in the specification table. No digit was changed.

| Sku | Name | OAL | Nose | Bearing | Boattail | Nose + bearing + boattail | Difference |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 30485 | 30 cal 208 gr LR Hybrid Target | 1.575 | 0.742 | 0.490 | 0.217 | 1.449 | −0.126 |
| 37379 | 375 cal 379 gr ELR Match Solid | 2.236 | 1.122 | 0.371 | 0.308 | 1.801 | −0.435 |
| 37407 | 375 cal 407 gr ELR Match Solid | 2.347 | 1.132 | 0.415 | 0.300 | 1.847 | −0.500 |
| 26403 | 6.5 mm 130 gr VLD Target | 1.310 | 0.735 | 0.445 | 0.223 | 1.403 | +0.093 |

The same tables print base to ogive: 0.788, 0.679, 0.715, and 0.719. On both solids, bearing surface plus boattail equals that base-to-ogive figure (0.371 + 0.308 = 0.679, and 0.415 + 0.300 = 0.715). Nose plus base to ogive is still 1.801 and 1.847, short of the printed OAL by 0.435 and 0.500. On the two jacketed bullets, bearing surface plus boattail is not the printed base to ogive, and nose plus base to ogive is not the printed OAL.

The other Berger records whose three segments miss overall length are off by at most 0.038 in, mostly about 0.02 in. Those were left as published.

## Answer

Berger prints these figures. They were not a transcription error. No length was changed.
