# Bergara has no component-bullet catalog

- Status: answered
- Date: 2026-10-27
- Topic: Licensing

## Question

Bergara was a candidate rifle-bullet source. Does it publish component bullets that can be committed?

## Context

Checked 2026-10-27. [bergara.online/us](https://www.bergara.online/us/) describes rifles and accessories. The shop sections are rifles, barrels, stocks, parts, apparel, and accessories. [other-products](https://www.bergara.online/us/other-products/) is accessories, apparel, and parts. The 2020 "Componentes y accesorios" PDF is trigger guards, bolt knobs, thread protectors, muzzle brakes, and magazines. It does not list component bullets.

The [terms of use](https://www.bergara.online/us/terms-of-use/) say site content is Bergara's property and is protected by copyright. They do not say specifications may not be copied. There is no component-bullet table to copy, so that clause was not used as a skip reason.

## Working assumption until answered

Commit no Bergara rows. Schema stays 1.2.0.

## Answer

Bergara does not publish a component-bullet catalog. No rows are committed. Schema stays 1.2.0.
