# Rocky Mountain Reloading in-house rifle bullets

- Status: answered
- Date: 2026-10-27
- Topic: Licensing

## Question

Rocky Mountain Reloading sells its own rifle bullets and also resells other makers' pulled and seconds bullets. May the in-house rifle rows be committed?

## Context

Checked 2026-10-27. The terms at [rmrbullets.com/terms-and-conditions](https://www.rmrbullets.com/terms-and-conditions/) say the site may contain other proprietary notices whose terms must be followed. They do not themselves forbid copying specifications. The product-page footer is `© 2026 RMR Bullets. All Rights Reserved.` That is a copyright notice. Product pages checked for the in-house rifle bullets do not add a reproduction ban.

The in-house category is the bullets RMR says it makes. Rifle rows there are two 55 grain .224 FMJ bullets (with and without a cannelure), a 69 grain BTHP 3GH, and a 75 grain BTHP 3GH. The category label prints `.224" (5.56mm)`. The 69 grain description says to expect a BC about .320-.335 depending on velocity and does not name a drag model. The 75 grain description mentions BC and does not print a number. Factory-seconds listings of the same bullets are not separate rows. Pistol bullets are omitted. Pulled Sierra, Magtech, Federal, Winchester, Nosler, and unnamed other-maker seconds are omitted because the page says RMR did not make them.

## Working assumption until answered

Commit the four in-house rifle bullets. Leave the BC range in notes. Schema stays 1.2.0.

## Answer

The terms page does not forbid copying specifications. The four in-house rifle bullets are committed. Other makers' bullets and pistol bullets are not. Schema stays 1.2.0.
