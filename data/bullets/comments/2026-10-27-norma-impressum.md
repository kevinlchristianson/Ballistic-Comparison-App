# Norma impressum forbids using site texts

- Status: answered
- Date: 2026-10-27
- Topic: Licensing

## Question

Norma publishes component-bullet pages under hunting bullets and shooting bullets. May those specifications be committed?

## Context

Checked 2026-10-27. The product sitemap lists component bullets at `https://www.norma-ammunition.com/products/components/hunting-bullets` and `https://www.norma-ammunition.com/products/components/shooting-bullets`. A product page prints a ballistic coefficient with a G1 and sometimes a G7 label, plus a weight in grams and grains. The caliber label often includes a decimal inch, such as `6,5 mm (.264)`.

The [impressum](https://www.norma-ammunition.com/about-norma/impressum) says:

> All texts, software and images on this homepage are the property of NORMA and may not be used without our express written permission. The data in the download sections can be used for non-commercial personal use.

There is no separate terms-of-use page. The privacy policy does not grant a license to copy product specifications. No Norma rows were fetched.

## Working assumption until answered

Leave Norma out until the project owner records written permission in BOTS.md.

## Answer

Agreed. No Norma rows unless the project owner records written permission in BOTS.md. The adapter raises `TermsForbid` and writes no file.
