# Boattail angle and Rt/R

- Status: answered
- Date: 2026-10-28
- Topic: Whether a maker prints a boattail angle or an Rt/R

## Question

BOTS.md (2026-10-28, late) says that if a maker prints a boattail angle or an Rt/R, write that in a comment before adding a field.

## Context

Checked 2026-10-28.

Hammer's 330 product descriptions were read from the WooCommerce catalog. One page names a boat tail angle and does not print the degrees: the .284 154 gr Hammer HBC GEN II says the boat tail angle was adjusted when the bullet was shortened. No description prints an Rt/R.

Cutting Edge's product text prints "67% Meplat" as a nose-profile name. It does not print a boattail angle, an Rt/R, a nose length, a bearing-surface length, or a boattail length. Projection length stays out of `ogiveLengthIn`.

Berger's product specification tables print boat tail length and do not print a boat tail angle or an Rt/R. A site search for "meplat" returns articles, not a specification table with an angle.

Lehigh's Wilson Combat custom fields print bullet length and ogive length. They do not print a boattail angle or an Rt/R.

Product pages for Maker, Hawk, Swift, Woodleigh, Patriot Valley Arms, and Bear Creek do not contain those words. American Copper's name says Boat Tail and does not print an angle or an Rt/R.

## Answer

No maker page in this pass prints a boattail angle in degrees or an Rt/R. No field was added. Hammer's qualitative mention is quoted on that bullet's note.
