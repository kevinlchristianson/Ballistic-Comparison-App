# 6x6.8 SPC and 6 WOA were not added

- Status: answered
- Date: 2026-10-27
- Topic: Cartridges

## Question

Add the 6x6.8 SPC wildcat, also called the 6 WOA, with both names as aliases, if a permitted page prints its dimensions.

## Context

Checked `data/cartridges/cartridges.json` and `data/cartridges/bots/loads.json` on 2026-10-27. Neither file has `6 WOA`, `6mm WOA`, `6x6.8`, `6x6.8 SPC`, or `6mm SPC`. The parent, `6.8mm Remington SPC`, is already in the owner list. No row was added to `data/cartridges/bots/bullets.json`.

The names are not aliases of one cartridge on the pages that discuss both. A Shooters' Forum post says:

> The main difference is the WOA has a 30 degree shoulder vs a 23 degree shoulder for the 6X6.8.

Source: <https://forum.accurateshooter.com/threads/6mm-6-8-rem-spc-vs-6-woa.3902793/>, retrieved 2026-10-27. The same post says the 6x6.8 is a neck-down with no fire-forming. Forum terms for that site were already accepted in `data/loads/comments/2026-10-27-community-forum-terms.md`, with the copyright-retention sentence flagged. Those two angles are the only dimensions that post prints. It does not print bullet diameter, case length, rim diameter, max COAL, water capacity, or pressure.

A cartridge record needs a printed bullet diameter and case length. Neither was found on a page whose terms allow copying, so the two names were not stored as one wildcat and neither was added alone.

### What is missing

| Field | 6x6.8 | 6 WOA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bullet diameter | not printed | not printed |
| Case length | not printed | not printed |
| Shoulder angle | 23° in the forum post above | 30° in the same post |
| Rim, max COAL, neck, capacity, pressure | not printed | not printed |

"6mm" in the name was not turned into a diameter. The parent 6.8 SPC dimensions were not copied onto either wildcat.

### Pages checked that do not supply a dimension set

- White Oak Armament is the shop the forum posts name for 6 WOA dies and barrels. A catalog search for `6 WOA barrel` on whiteoakarmament.com returned no product. A direct request on 2026-10-27 returned HTTP 403, so no White Oak dimension page was read.
- Wilson Combat publishes 6.8mm SPC rifles and ammunition, not a 6 WOA or 6x6.8 wildcat. Those pages were not used.
- CH4D is named in want-to-buy posts as a die maker for 6x6.8. The listings found are sales posts and a 6.8 SPC Improved die, not a printed case drawing.
- Pacific Tool & Gauge, <https://www.pacifictoolandgauge.com/Terms-and-Conditions_ep_2-1.html>: "The copying, reproduction, use, modification or publication by you of any such matters or any part of the Site is strictly prohibited, without our express prior written permission." No 6 WOA or 6x6.8 drawing was copied. A third-party reamer inventory that names PTG and JGS tools does not list either cartridge.
- JGS Precision does not publish custom prints on the site. Their FAQ says custom prints stay proprietary until the owner releases them.
- Manson, Whidden, Starline, Nosler, Alpha Munitions, Peterson Cartridge, and Weatherby each forbid copying site content without written permission. Their catalogs were not used. Peterson's drawings list does include other wildcats, and that list was not copied.
- SAAMI drawings, C.I.P. sheets, and The Ballistic Assistant's SAAMI reprint stay out, as in `2026-10-27-cartridge-gaps.md`. The parent 6.8 SPC is already in the library.

## Working assumption until answered

Leave both names out until a permitted page prints bullet diameter and case length for one of them. If that page is only one of the two, add that cartridge alone. Do not alias 6 WOA to 6x6.8 unless a page says they are the same cartridge.

## Answer

The project rule is not to commit a source whose terms forbid copying, and not to add a cartridge without a printed bullet diameter and case length. No such page was found. The forum post above is the only permitted dimension, and it is a shoulder angle that distinguishes the two names.
