# Loose match drops Lapua from 6x47 Lapua

- Status: open
- Date: 2026-10-29
- Topic: `6 x 47mm` resolves to the new `6x47-lapua` record

## Question

`looseAliasKey` drops the maker word Lapua. The new load-bot record `6x47-lapua` (name `6x47 Lapua`) therefore also matches bare `6x47` and Hodgdon's `6 x 47mm`. Can the loose match keep a name unmatched when dropping the maker word lands on a key another published cartridge uses?

## Context

Hodgdon lists two cartridges. `6X47MM Lapua` is 160 rows, twist 1:8, COL 2.440, trim 1.840, diameter .243. `6 x 47mm` is 50 rows, twist 1:14, COL 2.400, trim 1.840, diameter .243. 4D rents both a 6mm x 47 Lapua reamer and a separate 6mm x 47 on the .222 Remington Magnum. The load-bot record is the Lapua case, from the 4D Berger drawing (case 1.850) and PTG groove .2430. It is not `6x47-lapua-ai`.

The Lapua-named strings should resolve to `6x47-lapua`. Bare `6x47` was one of the community names for that case. Hodgdon's separate `6 x 47mm` entry is the one that may be the other case. An alias cannot split them, because both keys become `6x47` once Lapua is dropped.

## Working assumption until answered

Leave the record in place. All four strings resolve to `6x47-lapua`. No second record is added for `6 x 47mm` without a drawing that prints a different case length.
